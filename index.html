Composite Decking

No sanding, no staining, no annual treatment. Composite decking costs more up front than timber, but it earns that back in the weekends you're not spending on it — just a clean, modern deck that looks the same in ten years as it does on day one.

Timber Decking

The classic choice, and still the right one for a lot of gardens. Timber decking has a warmth and grain that composite can't fake, built on solid, properly spaced joists so it stays firm underfoot — and it's kinder to your budget from the start.

FAQs

Common questions, straight answers.

Anything else, just ask when you get in touch — happy to talk through the specifics of your garden.

Most domestic fencing jobs are completed within 1–3 days, depending on the length and ground conditions. Decking projects usually take 2–5 days. We'll give you an accurate timeframe as part of your free quote.

Most garden fencing and decking falls under permitted development and won't need planning permission — but there are exceptions around fence height, boundary rules and raised decking. We're happy to advise on your specific project when we quote.

Timber decking has a warm, natural look but needs regular staining or oiling to stay protected. Composite decking costs more up front but needs virtually no maintenance — no rotting, warping or annual treatment.

We supply and source all materials as standard — timber, composite boards, posts, fixings and gravel boards are all included in your quote.

Yes — we're fully insured for all fencing and decking work. Every job is carried out by an NVQ qualified tradesperson with over 20 years' experience, so it's done right the first time.

We are based closest to Medway and regularly provide fencing and decking across Maidstone, Sittingbourne, Gravesend and the surrounding Kent areas. If you're just outside these locations, get in touch anyway — we may still be able to help.
