Our Story

Twenty years of fencing and decking, done properly.

Parkwood Fencing & Decking has been building fences, decks and garden structures across Medway and Kent for more than two decades. It's a hands-on trade — every job is measured, quoted and installed by Mark, an NVQ qualified tradesperson who's seen just about every ground condition, boundary quirk and Kent weather curveball a garden can throw up.

We've stayed small and personal on purpose. No call centre, no rotating crew of unknown subcontractors — just a trusted, familiar team who turn up when they say they will, do the job right the first time, and leave your garden tidier than they found it.

How We Work

What you can expect from us.

01

Straight-talking quotes

Clear, honest pricing explained in plain English and agreed before any work starts — no jargon, no hidden extras once the job's underway.
