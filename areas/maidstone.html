Built For Maidstone Gardens

Every garden's a bit different around here.

Maidstone's a mixed bag when it comes to gardens. Newer estates around Allington and Bearsted tend to have flatter, smaller plots — great for clean composite decking and low-maintenance boundary fencing. Head further out towards Loose, Coxheath or Boughton Monchelsea, closer to the Greensand Ridge, and it's a different story — sloped, terraced gardens that need proper retaining work, stepped fence panels or a raised decking frame built to handle the ground properly.

We've worked both ends of that range, so whichever kind of plot you've got, we'll know what it actually needs — not just what's easiest to fit.
