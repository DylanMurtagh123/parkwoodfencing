Pergolas
Shape, Shade & Structure

A pergola turns empty space into somewhere to sit.

A pergola doesn't need walls or a full roof to change how a garden feels — just posts, beams and a bit of structure overhead to turn an open patio or deck into a defined space, whether that's for climbing plants to grow into, string lights for evening use, or simply a frame that draws the eye toward where you sit.

We build pergolas both freestanding and attached directly to the house, usually running off an existing deck or patio so the whole space reads as one considered design rather than two separate additions.

  • ✓Attached or freestanding designs
  • ✓Solid, concrete-set posts & beams
  • ✓Slatted or solid roof options
  • ✓Built into a decking project, or standalone
  • ✓Ready for climbing plants or string lighting
  • ✓Matched timber finish to your fencing or decking

Which Pergola Fits Your Garden?

Styles we build.

Not sure what would suit your space? Get in touch and we'll talk it through when we quote.

Attached Pergolas

Fixed to the house wall, extending your outdoor living space directly off a doorway or bifold doors.

Freestanding Pergolas

Built as a standalone structure anywhere in the garden, over a patio, deck or seating area.

Pergolas Over Decking

Built directly into a decking project as one combined structure, rather than added on as an afterthought.

Trellis & Climbing-Plant Sides

Open-sided panels for jasmine, wisteria or climbing roses to grow into over time.

Slatted Roof

The classic pergola look — timber slats laid across the top for dappled, partial shade.

Solid Roof

A solid or polycarbonate panel roof for fuller weather protection, while keeping the open-sided pergola feel.

The Structural Side

What goes into a pergola.

The parts underneath are what decide whether it's still standing square in ten years.

Concrete-Set Posts

Set below ground in concrete, the same way we set fence posts, so the structure doesn't shift or lean over time.

Solid Beam & Rafter Construction

Properly sized beams and rafters, jointed and fixed to carry the weight of climbing plants, lighting or a solid roof panel.

Roof Options

Classic slatted timber for dappled shade, or a solid or polycarbonate roof panel for more weather protection.

Matched Timber Finish

Stained or treated to match your existing fencing or decking, so it reads as one design rather than an add-on.
