Which Style Is Right For You?

Types of fencing we install.

Not sure which is right for your garden? Get in touch and we'll talk it through when we quote.

Closeboard Fencing

Our most popular boundary fence. Vertical feather-edge boards on arris rails, set into concrete posts with gravel boards — solid, private and built to handle Kent's exposed, windy gardens.

Panel & Overlap Fencing

A quicker, more budget-friendly option using pre-made overlap or waney-edge panels. A good fit for boundaries that need to look tidy from day one without the cost of closeboard.

Feather-Edge Fencing

Individually fixed feather-edge boards for a tighter, more solid finish than standard panels — often specified alongside closeboard for a premium boundary.

Decorative & Trellis-Top Fencing

Scalloped or trellis-topped panels that soften a boundary without losing privacy — popular for front gardens and anywhere a fence needs to look as good as it works.

Slatted & Privacy Screening

Modern horizontal slatted screens for extra privacy around a patio, hot tub or seating area, without boxing the whole garden in.

Picket & Low-Level Fencing

Low, open-style fencing for front gardens and boundaries that need definition rather than full privacy.
