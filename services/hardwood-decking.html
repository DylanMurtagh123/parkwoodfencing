Hardwood
The Premium Choice

Decking that ages beautifully, not badly.

Hardwood is denser and naturally more durable than standard softwood timber, with a high natural oil content that resists rot and insect damage without needing the same level of chemical treatment. It costs more up front, but it's the closest thing to a lifetime deck we install.

Left untreated, it weathers to an attractive silver-grey over time — similar to cedar. If you'd rather keep the rich, deep tone it starts with, an occasional oil does the job. Either way, the timber itself is built to handle whatever the Kent weather throws at it.

  • ✓Dense, naturally durable timber
  • ✓Resists rot & insect damage naturally
  • ✓Built to last decades, not years
  • ✓Ages to a striking silver-grey, or stays rich with oil
  • ✓Smooth-planed or grooved board finishes
  • ✓Solid subframe — no give, no bounce

Finishes & Layouts

Styles we build.

We can bring sample boards to your quote so you can see the tone and grain before you commit.

Oiled Hardwood Finish

An annual oil keeps the rich, deep original tone of the timber looking as good as day one — a favourite for decking close to the house.

Naturally Weathered Finish

Left untreated, hardwood weathers to a striking silver-grey over time — no maintenance required, just a different look.

Smooth-Planed Boards

A clean, refined finish underfoot — popular for decking close to the house or anywhere bare feet are likely.

Grooved & Reeded Boards

Ridged boards that add extra grip when wet, and a bit more visual texture than a flat smooth finish.

Raised & Multi-Level Decking

Built up on a solid subframe to level out sloped ground, or stepped down in stages for a multi-level layout.

Steps, Edging & Trims

Hardwood steps and edging finished to match the decking itself, for a job that looks considered right to the last board.

Hardwood, Timber Or Composite?

An honest comparison, not just a sales pitch.

All three are good options — it depends what you're optimising for. We install all of them, so we've no reason to steer you toward the pricier one.

Choose Hardwood If…

You want the richest, most premium natural look, exceptional durability without heavy chemical treatment, and you're happy to invest more up front for a deck that can outlast standard softwood by decades.
