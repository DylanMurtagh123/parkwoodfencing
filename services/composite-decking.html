Composite
Low Maintenance, Built To Last

Composite decking that doesn't ask for maintenance.

No sanding, no staining, no annual treatment. Composite decking costs more up front than timber, but it earns that back in the weekends you're not spending on it — just a clean, modern deck that looks the same in ten years as it does on day one.

It's also a genuinely good fit for a British garden. English weather is damp far more often than it's dry, and that's exactly the environment where timber decking starts to green, warp and need attention. Composite simply shrugs it off.

Boards & Finishes

Colours and styles we install.

We can bring sample boards to your quote so you can see the finish against your own house and garden before you commit.

Stone Grey

Our most popular colour — a cool, contemporary grey that suits modern rear gardens and works with almost any brickwork.

Graphite & Charcoal

A deep, near-black tone for a bold, high-contrast look — especially striking against lighter stone, gravel or planting.

Golden Teak

A warmer, timber-toned composite for gardens that want the natural decking look without the natural decking maintenance.

Dual-Tone & Multi-Tonal Boards

Boards blended with subtle variation in tone, closer to the natural grain variation you'd get in real timber.

Grooved Reversible Boards

Two usable faces — a grooved side for extra grip, a smooth side for a cleaner look — so you can choose the finish, or flip it later.

Raised & Split-Level Decking

Composite decking built up on a subframe to level out a sloped garden, or stepped down in stages for a multi-level layout.

Composite Or Timber?

An honest comparison, not just a sales pitch.

Both are good options — it depends what you're optimising for. We install both, so we've no reason to steer you toward the pricier one.

Choose Composite If…

You want zero ongoing maintenance, you're not keen on annual staining, your deck is near a hot tub or pool, or you'd rather pay more up front than spend weekends on upkeep every year.

Choose Timber If…

You want the warmest, most natural look and feel, you'd prefer a lower upfront cost, or you don't mind an annual oil or stain to keep it looking its best.

Composite Decking FAQs

Common questions about composite decking.

Anything else, just ask when you get in touch — happy to talk through the specifics of your garden.

Composite boards are made from a mix of recycled wood fibre and recycled plastic, capped with a protective outer shell. That's what gives them the look of timber without the rotting, splintering or warping that comes with real wood over time.

Like any dark surface, darker boards can feel warm underfoot on a hot day, though generally no worse than stone paving. Our grooved boards give extra grip when wet, which is the bigger concern in a British garden — most customers find it noticeably less slippery than algae-prone timber.

The most popular options are Stone Grey, Graphite/Charcoal and Golden Teak, along with dual-tone boards for a more natural, blended finish. We can bring sample boards along to your quote so you can see them against your house and garden.

Most residential composite decking projects take 2–5 days, depending on the size of the area, ground preparation needed and whether it's a raised or split-level design. We'll give you an accurate timeframe as part of your free quote.

It costs more upfront, but there's no annual staining or oiling, and no re-treating every couple of years. For a lot of our customers, especially those who travel a lot or simply don't want another job on the to-do list, it pays for itself in the maintenance they never have to do.
