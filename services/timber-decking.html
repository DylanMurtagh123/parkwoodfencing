Timber
The Classic Choice

Timber decking, warm and natural, built by hand.

Still the right choice for a lot of gardens. Timber has a warmth and grain that composite can't fake, built on solid, properly spaced joists so it stays firm underfoot — and it's kinder to your budget from the start than composite.

The trade-off is a bit of upkeep: an oil or stain every year or two keeps it looking its best and protects it against Kent's damp winters. It's a straightforward job, and one a lot of our customers actually enjoy — a Saturday afternoon that keeps the deck looking exactly the way they wanted it in the first place.

  • ✓Natural warmth composite can't match
  • ✓More budget-friendly to install
  • ✓Built on solid, properly spaced joists
  • ✓Can be stained or oiled to match your garden
  • ✓Smooth-planed or grooved board finishes
  • ✓Raised, stepped & multi-level layouts

Boards & Layouts

Styles we build.

Not sure what would suit your garden? Get in touch and we'll talk it through when we quote.

Smooth-Planed Boards

A clean, refined finish underfoot — a popular choice for decking close to the house or anywhere bare feet are likely.

Grooved & Reeded Boards

Ridged boards that add extra grip when wet, and a bit more visual texture than a flat smooth finish.

Pressure-Treated Softwood

Our standard timber — treated to resist rot and insect damage, and takes stain or oil well for years of protection.

Raised & Multi-Level Decking

Built up on a proper joist frame to level out sloped ground, or stepped down in stages for a multi-level garden layout.

Built-In Seating & Planters

Bench seating, raised planters or storage built straight into the decking frame, rather than added on afterwards.

Balustrades & Steps

Timber balustrades, handrails and steps for raised decking — built to the right height and properly fixed.

Timber Or Composite?

An honest comparison, not just a sales pitch.

Both are good options — it depends what you're optimising for. We install both, so we've no reason to steer you toward the pricier one.

Choose Timber If…

You want the warmest, most natural look and feel, you'd prefer a lower upfront cost, or you don't mind an annual oil or stain to keep it looking its best.

Choose Composite If…

You want zero ongoing maintenance, you're not keen on annual staining, or you'd rather pay more up front than spend weekends on upkeep every year. See our composite decking page for more.
