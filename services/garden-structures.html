What Holds It All Together

What goes into every structure.

The parts you don't see are the ones that decide whether it's still standing square in ten years.

Solid, Level Foundations

A concrete pad, paving slabs or screw piles, chosen to suit your ground and the size of the build — never just set straight onto soil.

Weatherproof Roofing

Felt, EPDM rubber or shingle roofing, laid with a proper fall so water sheds away rather than pooling.

Cedar Or Treated Cladding

Natural weathering cedar, or pressure-treated softwood cladding for a more budget-conscious finish that still holds up.

Glazing & Doors

Bifold, sliding or French doors, single or double glazed depending on how you plan to use the space year-round.

From First Call To Finished Build

How it works.

01 · Free Site Visit

We look at your garden, talk through how you'll use the space, and check ground conditions — no obligation.

02 · Fixed Quote & Design

A clear, itemised quote covering foundation, structure, cladding, roofing and glazing — sized and specified to your garden.

03 · Foundation & Build

Foundation first, then frame, cladding, roofing and glazing — typically a few days for a shed, 1–3 weeks for a full garden room.

04 · Tidy Handover

Offcuts and packaging cleared away, the site tidied, and a full walk-through with you before we leave.
